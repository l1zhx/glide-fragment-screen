## AI for Science · Molecular Screening

I work on reproducible molecular screening workflows that connect scientific models, cheminformatics and computational evaluation.

### [AF3 Ligand Screen](https://github.com/l1zhx/af3-ligand-screen)

Protein–ligand screening with reusable receptor MSAs, Slurm orchestration, confidence auditing and structural review.

### [Glide Fragment Screen](https://github.com/l1zhx/glide-fragment-screen)

A β-catenin case study built around a reported ~60,000-compound Glide SP campaign: Top10k pose audits, exactly100 chemical groups, sequential score/LE selection, four-region enrichment and traceable manual review for subsequent swimming simulations. Current software includes public synthetic demos; private-data reruns remain separate.

The repositories include reproducible synthetic demonstrations and explicit distinctions between computational prioritization and biological validation.
